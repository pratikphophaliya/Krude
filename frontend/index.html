    KrudeAi Model

    Fine-tuned Llama 3.2 3B served locally by Ollama. If Ollama is down, a labelled keyword fallback scores headlines instead.

    Training Provenance & Limitations

    • Model: Llama 3.2 3B Instruct + LoRA (Ollama model Krude-risk)
    • Output: A 0–10 risk score only; the explanation shown with each score is rule-based
    • Training data: not included in this repository
    • Inference Input: Raw breaking news headline string (no corridor leak at inference)
    • Serving Stack: Ollama (llama.cpp), F16 weights, CUDA 12 on the local GPU
    Known Architectural Trade-off: Model was trained on headline + corridor + event type, but infers from headline alone in production to support un-categorized breaking news.

    Validation Metrics

    Not reported. Earlier versions showed MAE 0.72 vs 1.84 for a TF-IDF baseline and a 199-row confusion matrix. The labelled holdout behind those numbers is not in this repository, so they cannot be reproduced and have been removed. Every live score on this page names the engine that produced it (fine-tuned model or keyword fallback).

    Terms & Conditions

    1. Purpose: Krude is an open-source energy security intelligence digital twin designed for geopolitical risk evaluation, crude maritime supply routing, and sovereign Strategic Petroleum Reserve (ISPRL) scenario modeling.

    2. Empirical Models: Risk indices, freight calculations, and LP optimization outputs are calibrated from public maritime datasets (PPAC, Vortexa, AIS, GDELT, and NewsAPI) for research and decision-support.

    3. Open Source: Developed under the MIT license for academic and empirical supply chain research.

    Privacy Policy

    1. Data Collection: Krude does not collect, sell, or monetize any personal telemetry or tracking cookies.

    2. Local Inference: Custom inference sandbox requests run locally or against specified local endpoints without remote data logging.

    3. Third-Party Feeds: Maritime news feeds are retrieved securely using authenticated public news APIs directly from your configured environment.
